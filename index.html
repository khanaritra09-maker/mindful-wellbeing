      YOUR SETTINGS & PRIVACY

      Your data, your choice

      Mindful collects your email and display name to provide your account. Mood, intensity, and optional reflections help show your own saved history; journal entries, tags, linked check-ins, and completed activities are kept only when you choose to save them. These personal entries remain in this browser and are not sent to an AI service. The optional voice recorder previews audio on this device and never uploads it. Browser speech-to-text may use your browserâ€™s speech service; check its privacy settings before choosing that feature.

      Admin dataset recordings are a separate feature: the speaker must agree before recording, then separately agree to upload and retain a sample. Dataset audio is private and restricted to authorized administrators. Account data is retained until you delete it. Admin audit records are retained for at least 365 days; voice dataset samples remain until deleted or consent is withdrawn.

      Privacy preferences

      About Mindful

      Mindful is a space for personal check-ins, writing, and optional wellbeing activities. It offers reflection tools, not diagnosis or treatment.

      Privacy policy

      Account email and display name are stored by the Mindful service. Mood history, journal entries, and activity history are currently stored in this browser under your signed-in account profile; they are not yet synced to the server. Reminders and these preferences are also stored on this device. Export browser data before clearing browser storage.

      Mindful does not send journal content to an external AI service. Speech-to-text is optional and may use your browser provider when you give per-use consent. Personal voice recordings are previewed locally and are not uploaded. Admin dataset recordings use a separate consent flow and private admin access. Account deletion removes your account and server records; deidentified audit records may remain for security operations.

      Terms of use

      Use Mindful for your own voluntary reflection. You decide whether to save entries, enable reminders, or use voice features. Do not submit recordings or personal information that you do not have permission to use. Activities and suggestions are optional and are not a substitute for professional or emergency support.
